[SHOW NOTES]
Eckhart Tolle is a world-revered spiritual teacher and author known for sharing practical tools that allow people to experience life more richly. We discuss, and Eckhart demonstrates, how you can access greater inner peace, authenticity, creativity, and connection in your everyday life. Eckhart also shares tools for quieting compulsive thinking, accessing inner stillness, and adopting a helpful mental vantage point when life gets challenging. We also discuss how to balance your pursuit of external goals with your spiritual growth and relationships and, in doing so, foster a deeper sense of well-being.
