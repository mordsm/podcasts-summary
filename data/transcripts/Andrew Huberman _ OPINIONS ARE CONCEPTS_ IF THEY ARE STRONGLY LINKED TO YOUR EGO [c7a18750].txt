Eckhart Tolle- world revered teacher on consciousness, explains. On the Huberman Lab podcast out now.

On the podcast, Tolle also explains some practices that you can use to observe where your ego starts and stops. Ego is important for striving, but if it’s too strongly attached to certain concepts, it makes you vulnerable to be offended by anything/one that disagrees with you.

In some ways, feeling flattered is just as dangerous/a reflection of a concept attached ego. (If people agreeing with you makes you feel too good, it’s also a sign that your ego is too strongly tethered to concepts.)

Eckhart explains that the goal is to not be detached or to meditate all the time, but rather to be able to understand why certain things bother you and to see the distinction between concepts linked to ego and concepts as a separate thing.

NOTE: Most of the episode focuses on practical tools to lean into life with more presence and calm and to experience relationships, nature, and your internal landscape with more peace and joy.
