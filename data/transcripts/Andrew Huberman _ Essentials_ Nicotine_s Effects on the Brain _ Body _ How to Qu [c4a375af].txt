[SHOW NOTES]
In this Huberman Lab Essentials episode, I explain how nicotine affects the brain and body, including how it increases dopamine, acetylcholine and norepinephrine to boost motivation, focus and alertness, suppresses appetite and raises heart rate and blood pressure. I distinguish nicotine itself from its delivery methods — smoking, vaping, dipping and snuffing — and explain their serious health risks, why vaping is especially habit-forming and why quitting is so difficult. I also discuss science-based tools to quit smoking or vaping, including clinical hypnosis, prescription medication and nicotine replacement strategies, and how to get through the critical first week of withdrawal.
