Today I'm testing the Thermal Master THOR002 handheld thermal camera in real-world situations.

Instead of only testing electronics, I also took the THOR002 outside at night to see what a thermal camera can actually detect in complete darkness.

In this video I test:
- Finding birds and mice in the garden at night
- Detecting heat signatures in complete darkness
- Inspecting heat distribution around a refrigerator
- Finding hot-water pipes hidden inside a wall
- Checking PCBs, power supplies and electronic components
- Testing thermal viewing modes and color palettes
- Finding hot spots, cold spots and temperature differences

One of the most interesting things about a thermal camera is that it does not rely on visible light like a normal camera. It detects differences in thermal radiation, allowing us to see warm objects, animals, pipes and equipment even in darkness.

The Thermal Master THOR002 features a 256×192 thermal sensor, image enhancement up to 512×384, a 25Hz thermal frame rate and a measurement range from -20°C to 550°C.
